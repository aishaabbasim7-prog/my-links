Privacy Policy

This policy is designed to comply with the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR.

Last Updated: May 2024

Welcome to The Aesthetic Nest. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how we operate.

1. Data Controller

For the purpose of UK GDPR, the data controller for this website is The Aesthetic Nest (operated via GitHub Pages). As we do not collect names or emails, we do not typically store personal identifying information (PII).

2. Affiliate Disclosure (UK Compliance)

The Aesthetic Nest is an affiliate marketing website. We provide links to third-party retailers. If you click a link and make a purchase, we may receive a commission.

  • All affiliate links are clearly marked with "Buy Now" or #ad.
  • We do not process your payments; all transactions happen on the retailer's secure website (e.g., Amazon.co.uk).

3. Cookies & Tracking

In accordance with the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), please note:

  • Affiliate Cookies: When you click a link, a small file (cookie) is placed on your device by the retailer to track the sale for 24-90 days.
  • Pinterest Tag: We use a Pinterest verification tag to link our website to our UK Pinterest business account.

4. Your Legal Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to request access to any data we might have (though we aim to collect none). You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) if you have concerns about how your data is handled.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us via our Pinterest Profile: The Aesthetic Nest.
